# Documentation for Digital Prototype 2

# Original README
## What are we making?
We are making a fast-paced platformer that features explosions that bounce you forward, and gives you a score based on how far you can travel in a certain amount of time.

### What question are we trying to answer with our prototype?
We would like to see if a parkour game with a few basic elements fun to play over and over again.

#### Why?
If we can make a small gameplay loop that players find rewarding, we won't have to rely on visuals to keep the player engaged over countless hours.

### What kind of experience do we envision?
One where players can compete with each other to see how far they can go, using the games movement and mechanics to push the limits of whats possible on repeat attempts.

### How is it innovative or experimental?
It is experimental as we are making a platformer that does not have a lot of convoluted mechanics, but still has high reply value. Players will be able to choose how fast they go, but will reward going faster.

# Concept and Final Version
We initially had the idea of making a platformer where the player would be unable to see when they are moving, making the player have to remember the layout of the map.
We got rid of this idea as we realized players would be constantly starting and stopping to be able to see, and it wouldn't be a fun experience.

![](https://cdn.discordapp.com/attachments/1472224430011977781/1565815519255462108/image.png?ex=6ae646d7&is=6ae4f557&hm=27fabcfa06f81c1e8569e7e327cef45380db41ed473a21f16b001fa5645c511a)

We changed it to a game where the player would be able to bounce and fly through the level using bombs. Our idea was that players could compete against each other to get as far as possible in a certain amount of time. 

The timer adds much needed pressure, otherwise players would want to take their time getting through the level. Forcing players to act and move quickly through the level. The bombs add movement tech, as players can find ways to maximize their distance with each explosion.

![](https://cdn.discordapp.com/attachments/1472224430011977781/1565818030414635068/CameraPan.png?ex=6ae6492d&is=6ae4f7ad&hm=29d4d4315fd70ba7bb04e6a10335b6e6262f5fca24f17c742fd516297f160b8f)
We wanted to make it so that the camera would zoom out when the player blasts, so that they can see the rest of the level and where they are going to land, but it was not able to be completed in time.

In the final version, the map was procedurally generated to allow for more replayability. The mechanics that we implemented were fun, and mostly went according to plan. We were not able to add everything we wanted, like the camera zooming, but we still got most of it.
